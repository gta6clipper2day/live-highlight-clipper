Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Live Highlight Clipper runs locally on the operator's own computer. It is used only by its operator.

Data from connected platforms

When the operator connects their TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as an account ID and display name). This data is stored only on the operator's computer and is used solely to publish the operator's clips to their own account. It is never sold or shared with third parties.

Live stream data

The app reads public live chat messages and public stream audio/video temporarily to detect highlight moments. Chat messages are kept in memory only and are not stored or shared. Video segments are deleted automatically after a few minutes unless they become part of a clip.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform; local tokens can be deleted by removing the app's local files.
