Live Highlight Clipper ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool operated by a single individual for their own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

The app only posts content to accounts that the operator owns and has explicitly authorized through the official login flows of TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. Authorization can be revoked at any time in the respective platform's settings.

Clips are short excerpts of public live streams. Each post names the original streamer and links to their channel. Rights holders who want a clip removed can request removal and it will be deleted promptly.
